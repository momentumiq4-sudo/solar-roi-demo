Assumptions in use

Calculation steps

  • Caps: self-consumed energy cannot exceed annual usage, and savings in year n cannot exceed the annual bill escalated at the same rate (usage held constant). Excess production earns $0 credit here. A bill is not the same as energy use: demand and fixed charges are not separable without the tariff.
  • Payback is searched only inside the selected period; if the cumulative net cash flow stays negative the result reads “Not reached”, never a projected figure.
  • Not modeled: demand charges, time-of-use or net-metering rules, inverter replacement, financing cost, taxes, depreciation, discounting, roof repair.

Needs review by a qualified solar professional

  • Power density and usable area (setbacks, obstructions, structure, condition).
  • Annual production per kW (shading, tilt, azimuth, soiling, losses).
  • Annual kWh versus bill (demand/fixed charges), the effective marginal rate, and tariff structure; self-consumption and export credit.
  • Installed cost and scope; maintenance and inverter replacement.
  • Escalation and degradation rates.
  • Incentive eligibility, amounts, deadlines, and interconnection requirements.
